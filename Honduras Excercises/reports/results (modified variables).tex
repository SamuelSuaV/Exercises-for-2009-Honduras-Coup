\noindent This report collects the synthetic-control exhibits for the
log-transform and first-difference models (\path{03b_PWT_SCM_implementation.do}'s
\texttt{models\_log}/\texttt{models\_diff}), companion to the main
\texttt{Results} report, which covers the level models. Same recipe throughout:
the fitted synthetic-control graphs, the Honduras-vs-synthetic prediction-error
gap, the in-space placebo distribution and its RMSPE histograms, and the
placebo overlay graphs (with and without poor-fitting outliers). Every model x
sample combination is reported for all four donor pools: World, Latin America,
South America, and Central America \& the Caribbean. Treatment year is 2009
(the coup); the pre-treatment fit window is 1993--2008 and the plotted window
1993--2019. \texttt{models\_diff} has no \texttt{\_yrs} variant (a single-year
first-difference predictor risks being missing for donors whose series doesn't
extend a year before 1993, which would abort \texttt{synth} outright) -- eight
models appear here per sample instead of the main report's five.

\noindent The in-space placebo p-values and donor-weight tables (the main
report's Sections 4 and 7) already tabulate every model -- level, log, and
first-difference -- side by side for comparison, so they are not repeated in
this report; see \texttt{results.tex}.

\section{Section 2: In-Space Placebo Computation}

For every sample x model, \texttt{04\_b} sec.\ 2 reruns \texttt{synth} once per
non-Honduras pool member as if that unit were treated (donor pool = the rest
of the pool, Honduras included as a donor), recording each unit's pre- and
post-2009 RMSPE. This step produces no figures or tables of its own -- it is
the data source for the RMSPE histograms (Section 3) and the placebo overlays
(Sections 5--6) below.
