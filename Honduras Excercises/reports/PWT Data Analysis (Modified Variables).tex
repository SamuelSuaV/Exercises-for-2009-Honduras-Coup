\noindent This report collects the log-transform and first-difference
counterparts of the "Comparison of averages" figures in the main
\texttt{PWT Data Analysis} report, produced by
\texttt{02b\_PWT\_data\_analysis.do} section~6 from the \texttt{log\_*}/
\texttt{diff\_*} variables built in \texttt{01b\_PWT\_data\_cleaning.do}
section~6. Each figure plots Honduras (thick cranberry line) against the
yearly cross-country mean of Central America \& the Caribbean, South
America, Latin America and the World; the dashed vertical line marks the
2009 coup. South America contributes no remittances series (no CEPAL
coverage).
