\noindent This report collects the synthetic-control exhibits produced by
\path{03b_PWT_SCM_implementation.do} and
\path{04_b_PWT_SCM_graphs.do} (in \path{Honduras Excercises/do/}):
the fitted synthetic-control graphs, the Honduras-vs-synthetic prediction-error
gap, the in-space placebo distribution and its RMSPE histograms and p-values,
the placebo overlay graphs (with and without poor-fitting outliers), and the
donor-weight composition tables. Every model x sample combination is reported
for all four donor pools: World, Latin America, South America, and Central
America \& the Caribbean. Treatment year is 2009 (the coup); the pre-treatment
fit window is 1993--2008 and the plotted window 1993--2019.

\section{Section 2: In-Space Placebo Computation}

For every sample x model, \texttt{04\_b} sec.\ 2 reruns \texttt{synth} once per
non-Honduras pool member as if that unit were treated (donor pool = the rest
of the pool, Honduras included as a donor), recording each unit's pre- and
post-2009 RMSPE. This step produces no figures or tables of its own -- it is
the data source for the RMSPE histograms (Section 3), the p-values
(Section 4), and the placebo overlays (Sections 5--6) below.
